# Brag Plan: Nexus Loop

## Invocation
Tone: product-focused, polished, emphasize the core problem, solution, key product differentiators, and real user value. Preset: polished. Voice: enabled, Kokoro af_heart. Landscape, 1920×1080, target 24 seconds. Music and restrained SFX enabled.

## Planning rubric
1. Nexus Loop turns a deployed support agent's logs into evidence-backed improvement decisions for a human operator.
2. Strongest claim: a successful tool call can return nothing while customer resolution falls; Nexus Loop exposes the cause behind the healthy status.
3. Visual hook: a green HTTP 200 signal contrasted with an unresolved customer outcome, then a precise operator finding.
4. Real UI: Findings queue, the silent-tool-contract finding, attributed configuration change, proposed fix, replay evidence, and Approve/Reject/Defer controls.
5. Minimum satisfying film: about 24 seconds with five semantic beats. Generated narration sets exact pacing.
6. Polished product film: controlled motion, substantial UI, clear problem→solution→value. No parody.
7. Warm, steady music under conversational narration; three quiet UI accents. Music volume ducks under voice and rises gently at the closing hold.
8. Caption: Nexus Loop connects agent logs to customer outcomes, identifies the change behind a regression, and puts evidence-backed improvement decisions in human hands.
9. User flow: open a finding → inspect cause and bounded fix with replay evidence → record a human verdict and copy a decision note.

## The angle
